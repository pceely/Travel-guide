# Peru

Peru is an Andean country on the Pacific Coast of South America, with a fascinating mix of cultures as shown in the food and architecture.  The Inca empire was centred in the Peruvian Andes mountains, with spectacular remains such as Machu Pichu.  There are many Spanish style towns and villages.  The food is an aaxing mixture of influences, being the home of the potato, more spanish style such as Pollo al la Braza, and Japanese influence such as raw fish ceviche. More info at [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peru)
